How we record a price change

Each time we re-check a tool on its official pricing page we save the listed prices. When the text changes, we compare it with the check before and sort the change into a kind. Real price moves: price went up, price went down, cheaper plan added, higher plan added, new plan added, plans reorganized, plan renamed at the same price, intro offer changed. Our listing updates (no price change): we added detail such as yearly prices, corrected our own listing, or switched currency. Before and after amounts are only the ones written on the official page; when old and new plans aren't like for like, we don't call it a rise or a cut. “No price change found” means none in our checks, so always confirm on the official page before you pay. Tools are re-checked at different times; each shows its own last-checked date.

Which AI tools raised their prices recently?

How do you know a price changed?

We copy the listed prices from each tool's official pricing page every time we re-check it and compare them with the previous check. The before and after amounts are the ones written on the page, with the date we read it and a link to the page.

Does “no price change found” mean the price is guaranteed?

No. It means none of our checks since we started tracking found a change. Each tool shows when we last checked it; always confirm on the official pricing page before you pay.

Should I pay yearly to avoid a price rise?

Yearly billing usually locks the price for the year and is often cheaper per month, but you pay up front and refunds vary. Our Monthly or yearly page shows the saving for each tool, and the refund guide shows which tools refund a yearly plan.
