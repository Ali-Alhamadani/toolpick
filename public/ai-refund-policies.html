Can I get a refund? AI subscription refund and cancel rules

Pick your tool and when you paid. We tell you if you're still inside its refund window, how to ask, and how to cancel. Rules for 22 AI tools, read on each official help page on 2026-10-27.

Billing

Choose a tool to see its refund rule

Refund rules change, and most say a refund “may” be given. We show what the official page says; the company decides. Not legal advice.

Before you subscribe

  • Shortest refund windows: Perplexity (24-hour monthly, 3-day yearly), Descript (48-hour), Gamma (3-day), Notion AI (3-day monthly, 30-day yearly). Try the free plan first, then test hard on day one.
  • Cancel early: ChatGPT (at least 24 hours before your next billing date), Claude (at least 24 hours before your next billing date), Gamma (at least 30 days before your renewal date), Ideogram (at least 5 days before your renewal date), Otter (at least 24 hours before your next billing date).
  • No refunds at all: Bolt, GitHub Copilot, Grammarly, Ideogram, Lovable, Otter, Synthesia. Start monthly, not yearly, until you're sure.
  • Pause instead of cancelling: Canva (up to 3 months), Jasper (30, 60 or 90 days), Otter (once) and HeyGen offer a pause.
  • You can lose things when you cancel: Synthesia removes access to your videos after the period ends, ElevenLabs and HeyGen unused credits go, Lovable freezes unused credits. Export first.

Bolt No refunds

Bolt doesn't give refunds. Cancelling only stops the next charge.

How to ask and how to cancel
  • Billing problem: Questions: support@bolt.new from your paid account's email.
  • How to cancel: Profile icon → Subscription → Billing details → Cancel subscription → follow the StackBlitz billing portal.
  • After you cancel: Your paid plan stays until the next billing period, then moves to Free. You can undo before then.

In their words: “Bolt subscriptions are non-refundable, as stated in our Terms of Service.”

FAQ

Can I get a refund on ChatGPT Plus?

Payments are generally non-refundable, but OpenAI says a discretionary refund may be available for an unused individual subscription if you ask within seven days of the charge, through the Help Center chat. In the EU, UK and Turkey you get a prorated refund if you cancel within 14 days. Read 2026-10-27.

Can I get a refund on Claude Pro?

Anthropic says all payments are non-refundable except where its Consumer Terms or the law say otherwise. You can check eligibility under Get help → Send us a message → Claude Refund Request. EEA and UK users can get a refund within 14 days, reduced for use. Read 2026-10-27.

Which AI tools give refunds?

Of the 22 tools we checked on 2026-10-27, 11 state a refund window or rule (for example Cursor and ElevenLabs: 14 days if unused; Runway: 30 days if unused or close to unused), 4 decide case by case, and 7 don't refund: Bolt, GitHub Copilot, Grammarly, Ideogram, Lovable, Otter, Synthesia.

Does cancelling give me my money back?

No. On every tool here, cancelling only stops the next renewal. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. A refund is a separate request, usually to support, and most have a short window.

I paid in the iPhone app. Who refunds me?

Apple. App Store subscriptions are refunded and cancelled through Apple (Google Play for Android), not by the tool itself. Several help pages say this, including ChatGPT, Notion, Canva and Perplexity.
