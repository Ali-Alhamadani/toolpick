How we work it out

  • Prices: copied from each official pricing page on the date shown on the card, before tax. Nothing is estimated.
  • Seats: totals are per-seat price × number of people (1–50).
  • Yearly: used only where the page shows a yearly price; otherwise the monthly price is used and the card says so. Rytr yearly is 20% off monthly (page wording). Google AI Pro yearly is $199.99/yr.
  • ChatGPT Go/Plus: US dollar amounts from our 2026-10-24 verified check (CMS-keyed on the live page today).
  • Not included: tax, add-ons, workflow credits beyond the plan, and custom/enterprise quotes.

Left out

  • Copy.ai Growth / Expansion / Scale: team GTM plans from $1,000/mo — different job and seat scale than a writing seat.
  • Jasper Business / Enterprise: contact-sales / custom pricing on the page.
  • Grammarly Enterprise: contact sales only.
  • ChatGPT Enterprise: contact sales; consumer Go/Plus US dollars are CMS-keyed on the pricing page today.
  • Notion Enterprise: custom pricing.
  • Google AI Ultra 20x: higher Ultra tier ($199.99/mo); calculator shows Ultra 5x as the published Ultra entry.

FAQ

What's the cheapest AI writing tool for one person?

Among tools on this page with a monthly price: Google AI Plus is $4.99/mo, Wordtune Advanced is $6.99/mo, ChatGPT Go is $8/mo (verified 2026-10-24), Rytr Lite is $9/mo, Claude Pro is $20/mo, Grammarly Pro is $30/mo, Jasper Pro is $69/seat/mo. Latest check among tools: 2026-11-06.

Why is ChatGPT's Go/Plus check date older?

chatgpt.com/pricing loads US dollar amounts for Go and Plus from CMS keys (not in the static HTML). We keep the last verified consumer prices from 2026-10-24. Pro “from $100” is confirmed in today's page HTML.

Is a free trial the same as a free plan?

No. Jasper's 7-day Pro trial and Wordtune's 3-day trial are trials, not ongoing free plans. Grammarly, Rytr, Claude, ChatGPT, Notion and Gemini list real free options.

How is Google AI Pro's yearly price worked out?

Google lists Google AI Pro at $19.99/mo or $199.99/yr on one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/ (checked 2026-11-06). $199.99 ÷ 12 ≈ $16.67/mo.
