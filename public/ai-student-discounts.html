Your claim checklist

Do these in order: most offers need your school email first. Time-limited offers renew at the regular price, so set a reminder.

    Only through your school

    These tools offer education pricing to institutions, not to individuals. Ask your school's IT or library.

    • Claude: Education plan: a university-wide plan bought by the institution for its students, faculty and staff. No individual student discount is listed. (claude.com, 2026-10-24)
    • Grammarly: Discounts for educational institutions (Grammarly for Education), not individual students. (grammarly.com, 2026-10-24)
    • Canva: Canva Campus for universities and colleges, set up by the institution. (canva.com, 2026-10-24)
    • Scite: A discount code if you recommend Scite to your institution (email them with your institution in cc). (scite.ai, 2026-10-24)

    No student discount (checked 2026-10-24)

    • ChatGPT: No student plan or discount on its pricing page today; it only mentions a Rosalind Academic Plan for life-sciences research. (chatgpt.com, 2026-10-24)
    • Cursor: No standing discount: “Anyone can get started with Cursor for free. To upgrade your access, look out for promotions at our on-campus and online events.” (cursor.com, 2026-10-24)
    • Warp: “At this time, Warp does not offer discounts for students, non-profits, or open-source teams.” It points to the Free plan instead. (warp.dev, 2026-10-24)
