Free plan limits, side by side

What each free plan really includes, its main limit, watermarks, commercial use, and the cheapest monthly upgrade. Every value read on the tool's official pricing, help or terms page on 2026-10-22; “not stated” means the page doesn't say.

Best free plan for…

  • Coding: GitHub Copilot, its free plan states a clear allowance: 2,000 completions a month.
  • Writing: Grammarly, free spelling and grammar checks, plus 100 AI prompts.
  • Images: Krea, 100 units every day, rather than a one-time grant.
  • Video: Descript, 60 media minutes a month with 720p export.
  • Audio: ElevenLabs, 10,000 credits every month across text to speech, sound effects and music.
  • Productivity & Meetings: Fathom, unlimited recordings and instant call summaries.
  • Presentations: Gamma, 400 credits at signup and PDF/PPTX export (with Gamma branding).
  • AI Assistants: Le Chat (Mistral), web and mobile access with image generation and 100+ connectors.
