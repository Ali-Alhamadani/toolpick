FAQ

What's the cheapest way to get good AI tools?

Start with free plans: 44 of the tools we rank have a free plan we confirmed on 2026-10-30. Pick your jobs in the planner with a $0 budget to see a free-only stack, then raise the budget to see which single upgrade helps most.

How does the planner choose?

For each job you pick, it looks at ToolPick's top 3 tools for that job. A paid plan of our #1 pick scores best, then #2 and #3, then free plans, then nothing. It tries every combination and returns the best one whose monthly total fits your budget. If one paid tool covers two of your jobs, you only pay for it once.

Are these prices current?

Every paid price was read on the tool's official pricing page on 2026-10-30, and each card quotes the words we saw. We use the cheapest paid (entry) plan. Taxes, add-on credits, promos for the first month and seat minimums aren't included. Prices change, so check the linked page before you buy.

What does “Yearly billing OK” change?

Many tools are cheaper per month when you pay for a year up front. With yearly billing on, the planner uses the lower per-month price and shows what you'd pay for the year. Some plans are only sold yearly, so they only appear with this option on.

Why is a tool missing?

We leave a paid plan out when its official page didn't show a clear US-dollar price on 2026-10-30. The list is on this page under “Left out tonight”. The tool's free plan can still be used if we confirmed it.

Is ChatGPT in the planner?

Yes, its free plan is (unlimited text chats on GPT-6, with limits on other features, per OpenAI's pricing page on 2026-10-30). Its paid plans aren't, because the pricing page didn't show amounts to our check tonight. For a paid everyday assistant the planner can pick Le Chat Pro ($14.99/mo) or Claude Pro ($20/mo, or $17/mo yearly).
