What will it really cost?

Pick a specific tool — or a whole category — then seats and monthly or annual billing. We multiply only the verified entry prices already in ToolPick's price table (checked on official pricing pages) and show the true monthly and yearly totals. Nothing is invented: no new prices, plan names, limits or features.

Seats only change the total where the verified entry price itself is stated per seat / per user / per member. Otherwise we say so and seats don't change the listed price.

Billing period

Annual means the per-month rate when billed yearly, from our table; the yearly total is that rate × 12.

How we work this out — and what we don't claim

True monthly cost is the verified entry rate for your billing choice (× seats only for a clearly per-seat entry price). True yearly cost is that monthly total × 12. Cheapest verified plan we can price means the lowest verified entry rate in our table for that tool — we only hold entry-tier rates, so we don't name plans or claim usage limits fit you; check the official pricing page for limits and higher tiers. Tools with no verified numeric entry price (“check site” or no rate in our table) are shown for reference, excluded from cheapest rankings, and never guessed. Category Best Value rule: first ToolPick's existing Best Value pick for that category if it has a verified price for your billing choice; otherwise the lowest verified entry with a verified free plan; otherwise the lowest verified entry. Free options use only our verified free-plan field — full free-tier limits are on the official pricing page.
