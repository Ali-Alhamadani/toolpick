How we work it out

Your team / month = price per seat × seats, using the seat minimum when your team is smaller (you pay for the minimum). Flat plans (one price for the whole workspace) don't multiply. A plan fee, like Windsurf's, is added once. “Billed yearly” uses the monthly rate the page shows when you pay for a year; per year = that × 12. If a page doesn't state a price for the billing you picked, or your team is above the plan's seat limit, we say so instead of guessing. Taxes are extra. Individual plans (Plus, Pro) aren't listed here: see AI subscription costs.

Not included yet

We leave a tool out when its official page doesn't let us state the team price clearly (read 2026-10-26):

  • Google Gemini: Gemini for teams comes inside Google Workspace plans; today's page showed introductory offers and footnoted prices, so we couldn't state a clean per-seat price.
  • HeyGen: Business is $149/mo plus $20 per seat, but the page doesn't say whether the $149 includes the first seat.
  • Jasper: Pro includes 1 seat; adding teammates needs the Business plan, which has custom pricing.
  • v0: the Team plan card showed two different per-user prices, so we couldn't tell which applies.
  • Zapier: Team is “from $69/month” for 25 users, but the page doesn't say which billing period that is.
  • Grammarly: the page shows Pro at $12 per member but didn't state the billing period in the text we could read.
  • ClickUp: its AI is a separate per-user add-on on top of the workspace plan.
  • Replit: its pricing page showed a security check today.

FAQ

How much does ChatGPT Business cost for a team?

$25 per seat per month billed monthly, or $20 billed yearly (standard seat), for teams of 2 to 200; a team of 5 pays $125/mo monthly or $1,200 a year billed yearly. Read on chatgpt.com/pricing on 2026-10-26.

Is Claude Team cheaper than ChatGPT Business?

On 2026-10-26 both standard seats cost the same: $25 per seat per month billed monthly or $20 billed yearly. Claude Team is for 2 to 150 seats, ChatGPT Business for 2 to 200.

Which AI coding assistant is cheapest for a team?

GitHub Copilot Business at $19 per user per month. For 5 developers that is $95/mo, vs Cursor Teams from $150/mo and Windsurf Teams $280/mo ($80 plan fee + $40 per seat), monthly prices read on 2026-10-26.

Do AI team plans have seat minimums?

Some do: ChatGPT Business, Claude Team, Fathom Team and Beautiful.ai Team start at 2 seats, so a solo buyer pays for 2. Others cap the team size, like Beautiful.ai (20), Otter Business (25 billed users) and Claude Team (150). The calculator applies these automatically.

Which team plans charge one price for everyone?

Lovable Business is one price for the whole workspace with unlimited users (from $50/mo, credits shared), so it gets cheaper per person as the team grows. Windsurf charges a fixed $80/mo plan fee on top of $40 per seat.
