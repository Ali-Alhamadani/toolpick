How we work it out

  • Prices: copied from each official pricing page on the date shown on the card, before tax. Nothing is estimated.
  • Seats: totals are per-seat price × number of people (1–50).
  • Yearly: used only where the page shows a yearly price; otherwise the monthly price is used and the card says so. Beautiful.ai Pro and SlidesAI paid plans are yearly-billed on the page we read.
  • Pitch (EUR): shown in euros and excluded from the USD cheapest ranking.
  • Not included: tax, add-ons, and credit packs beyond the plan price.

Left out

  • Synthesia / InVideo presentation modes: those products are sold mainly as video tools; this calculator covers presentation-first tools only.

FAQ

What's the cheapest AI presentation tool for one person?

Among USD tools on monthly billing where a monthly price is stated: Gamma Plus is $12/mo, Plus AI Basic is $15/mo, Canva Pro is $18/mo. Beautiful.ai Pro is $14.50/mo billed annually only. SlidesAI's paid plans are shown as yearly-billed rates. Pitch Plus is €10/mo (euros). Checked dates: Gamma/Canva 2026-10-24; others 2026-10-19.

Does Pitch count in the cheapest highlight?

No. Pitch is priced in euros on its official page, so we show it in € and keep it out of the USD cheapest ranking.

Is a free trial the same as a free plan?

No. Beautiful.ai's 14-day trial and Plus AI's 7-day trial (card required) are trials, not ongoing free plans. Gamma, Canva, Pitch and SlidesAI list real free plans.

How is Canva's yearly price worked out?

Canva Pro is $144/yr → $12/mo. Canva Business is $250/yr per person → about $20.83/mo. Both from canva.com/pricing (checked 2026-10-24).
