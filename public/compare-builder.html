How the “Ahead” marks and the verdict work

Each row is called by a fixed rule, using only facts read on the tool’s official page (each cell shows its source and checked date): price only when the tools do the same main job, on the same billing (monthly with monthly, or billed-yearly with billed-yearly), lighter tiers compared at their comparable tier; a free plan beats none; the bigger yearly saving; a free student plan, then a discount; the lower per-seat team price on the same billing; a stated refund window, then case by case, then none; not training on your data, then opt-in, then opt-out, then no opt-out. No row is called when a tool hasn’t been checked, and usage limits are shown but never ranked (each tool counts usage differently). The verdict tallies the rows you say matter to you; with none picked it tallies every row we could call. It never uses reviews, ratings or features we haven’t checked. How we pick.

FAQ

How does the ToolPick compare builder pick a winner?

Row by row, with fixed rules and only facts read on each tool's official page: the lower price on the same billing (only for tools that do the same main job), a free plan over none, the bigger yearly saving, a free student plan then a discount, the lower per-seat team price, a stated refund window, and not training on your data by default. You choose which rows matter; the builder tallies them. Rows are never called for tools we haven't checked.

Can I share a comparison?

Yes. The address updates as you pick, for example https://toolpick-ai.vercel.app/compare-builder.html?tools=chatgpt,claude&want=privacy,price, and the Copy share link button copies it.

Which facts are compared?

Price and cheapest checked plan, free plan limits, yearly billing saving, student deal, team per-seat price, refund window and how to cancel, whether your data trains their AI, and usage limits for 8 chat and coding assistants (checked 2026-11-03).
