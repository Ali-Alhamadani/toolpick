How the Best Value badge is chosen

Only when your picks do the same main job (from what each official site says it does). Then: if exactly one is ToolPick's Best Value pick for its category, it gets the badge; otherwise the badge goes to the tool that is cheaper on the same billing (monthly with monthly, or billed-yearly with billed-yearly, when all picks have that price). If your picks do different jobs, or their prices aren't checked on the same billing, no badge is given. Prices we can't compare (“check site”, euros, usage-only) are never ranked.
