Help me choose an AI tool

Answer 5 quick questions — what you need, your budget, billing and seats, and two must-haves we can actually verify — and get a ranked shortlist of 3–5 tools with verified prices, checked dates and a plain reason for each pick. We only ask about things in our verified data, so we don't ask about API access, commercial-use rights, privacy terms or skill level.

Step 1 of 5

1. What are you trying to do?
2. What is your monthly budget?
3. Billing period and seats

Seats only multiply the cost where the verified entry price itself says per seat / per user / per member / per person — the same rule as our cost calculator.

4. Must it have a real free plan?
5. Must annual billing be verified cheaper?
