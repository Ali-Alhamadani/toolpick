How we rank your picks

Which tools: for a job, our 3 picks for that job (from its “best AI tool for …” page) plus every tool with the same main job as the top pick (what it does comes from each tool’s own site). For a whole category, every tool in it. Budget: the cheapest verified paid plan for your billing, times your seats only when the official price is per seat; yearly uses the per-month price billed yearly (or the monthly price if no yearly price is checked). If a tool’s paid plan is over budget but it has a real free plan, it stays in, marked “free plan only”. Prices that are “check site”, in euros or usage-only never count as fitting a budget. Must-haves remove tools that don’t have it or that we haven’t checked. Ranking adds simple points you can see on each card: job fit (our #1 pick for the job 40, #2 36, #3 32, same main job 28; for a category, its Best Value pick 32, others 24), +12 for each nice-to-have it has, +4 if it partly has it, −6 if it doesn’t, −10 for “free plan only”, and for what matters most to you: +10 for our picks when you choose best fit, up to +20 for the lowest price (scaled between the cheapest and dearest option), +20 for not training on your data by default (+8 if you can switch it off), or +20 for a real free plan. Ties go to the lower price, then the name. Not ranked on: features, quality or ease of use — we haven’t tested tools hands-on, so read the tool page before you buy. Facts and prices come from official pages and can change; each shows the date we read it.

FAQ

How does Find my AI tool pick tools for me?

You choose a job (or a whole category), a budget with billing and seats, which facts are must-haves or nice-to-haves, and what matters most. It starts from ToolPick's 3 picks for that job plus tools with the same main job, removes tools that miss your budget or a must-have, then ranks the rest with visible points for job fit, each nice-to-have and your top priority. Every card explains its rank.

What if a tool hasn't been checked for privacy, refunds or student deals?

It shows as “not checked” and is never counted as having it. A must-have removes it (it appears under “Left out and why”); a nice-to-have gives it no points. So far we've read data-training rules for 22 tools, refund rules for 23 and student deals for 23.

Can I share my picks?

Yes. The address updates with your answers, for example https://toolpick-ai.vercel.app/help-me-choose.html?job=voiceovers&budget=10&m_free=must, and the Copy link button copies it.
