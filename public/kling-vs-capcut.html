FAQ

Is Kling cheaper than CapCut?

On entry paid plans, yes: Kling Standard is $8.8/mo (or $6.99 for the first month, 30% off once) and CapCut Pro is $19.99/mo. Kling Standard yearly is $79.20/yr; CapCut Pro yearly is $179.99/yr. (Kling checked 2026-11-02; CapCut checked 2026-11-06.)

Which has a free plan?

Both. Kling Basic is $0 (free credits not stated). CapCut has a free plan with basic editing and templates. CapCut's free plan is for editing; Kling's is for generative video.

Are Kling and CapCut the same kind of tool?

No. Kling is a text/image-to-video generator. CapCut is primarily a video editor with AI features. We only compare price and free options here — not quality or features.

Does CapCut list credit counts?

No. CapCut's pricing page shows Pro, Ultra and Teams dollar prices; it does not state a credit count we can compare with Kling's credits.

Which should I pick on price?

Pick Kling if you want the lowest generative-video subscription. Pick CapCut if you want an editor and a free plan for basic editing.
