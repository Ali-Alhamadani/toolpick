AI Subscription Audit — what could you stop paying for?

A 60-second check for subscription fatigue. Add the AI tools you pay for, confirm what you pay each month (our verified entry price is prefilled where we have one — edit it, your plan may differ), and get one clear action per tool: keep it, consider cancelling it, try its free tier first, or check a cheaper switch. Potential savings are counted once per tool, never twice.

Your privacy: this runs entirely in your browser. Your list and amounts are not sent to us and are not stored by us. They only appear in the share link if you copy one — so only share that link if you are happy for whoever opens it to see your list and amounts. Every price we show is copied from the tool's official pricing page with its checked date; free-tier limits are on that official page, not guessed here.

How each action is chosen — one per tool

In this order, so a tool is never counted twice: 1. Consider cancelling — you pay for 2 or more tools in the same category; we suggest keeping the category's existing ToolPick Best Value pick, or the lowest verified entry price if there is no such pick, and cancelling the others. 2. Try the free tier — the tool itself has a verified real free plan (free trials and one-time credits do not count). 3. Switch to check — another tool in the same category has a verified real free plan and a lower verified entry price than what you told us you pay; the potential saving we count is only the difference to that entry price, not your whole amount, so we do not overstate it. 4. Keep — none of the above applies on verified data. “Potential saving” always means if you act on every suggestion; tick or untick suggestions below to see your own plan total.
