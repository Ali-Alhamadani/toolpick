FAQ

Is it cheaper to pay for AI tools yearly?

Usually, yes - 60 of the 82 tools we track charge less per month on a yearly plan. The typical (median) discount is 21%, from 10% to 68%. But you pay the whole year upfront, and 25 of those tools say payments are non-refundable, so only pay yearly if you'll keep it past the break-even month.

What is the break-even month?

It's the month from which paying a year upfront costs no more than paying monthly. We work it out as the yearly price divided by the monthly price, rounded up. If you might cancel before that month, monthly is cheaper.

Which AI tools have the biggest yearly discount?

Biggest % off: Beautiful.ai (68%), Speechify (60%), Otter (51%), Predis.ai (50%), InVideo (50%), Taskade (50%). Most dollars saved a year: Predis.ai ($474), Botpress ($468), Beautiful.ai ($366), Chatbase ($360).

Which AI tools are monthly only?

On their pricing pages these show no yearly price for the cheapest paid plan: Captions, ChatGPT, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Google Gemini, Humata, Le Chat (Mistral), Lindy, Opus Clip, v0, Windsurf.

Where do the prices come from?

From each tool's official pricing page, read on 2026-11-13 with the page's exact words shown on every card. We compare the cheapest paid plan on the same plan, monthly vs billed yearly, in US dollars before tax. Intro offers (like a cheaper first month) are not counted. If a page shows only one price, no fixed price, or we couldn't read it, the card says so instead of guessing.
