How we work it out

We use the cheapest paid plan’s monthly price and its per-month price when billed yearly, both copied from the official pricing page with the date we checked. Upfront cost = yearly per-month price × 12. Yearly saving = monthly price × 12 − upfront cost. Break-even = upfront cost ÷ monthly price, rounded up: if you might cancel before that month, monthly is cheaper. Tools where we only checked one of the two prices are not ranked. Other plans can have different discounts — check the official page.
