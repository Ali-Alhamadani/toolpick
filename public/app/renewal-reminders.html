How it works

Each reminder repeats every month or every year on your renewal date, with an alert 1, 3 or 7 days before (set per reminder: the calendar file adds the alert, and Google Calendar links place the event on the alert day). Prices come only from each tool's official pricing page with its checked date. “Cheaper for the same job” compares only tools with the same main job on the same billing (monthly with monthly, yearly with yearly), and lighter entry tiers are compared at their comparable tier. Yearly prices are shown per month, plus the yearly charge. Reminders carry the price as of the day you download them; open your reminders link any time to re-check the latest prices.
