How we compare

  • Same unit. Everything is converted to characters, then minutes at the same rate.
  • Same model only for the verdict. ElevenLabs and Luma both sell ElevenLabs v3 speech, so we rank them. Runway doesn't name its voice model, so it's shown but not ranked.
  • Monthly billing only. Prices are the regular monthly price; ElevenLabs' $11 first month for Creator isn't used.
  • Only what the pricing page shows. Extra minutes are added only where a price is shown (ElevenLabs). Otherwise we say “More than the biggest plan” instead of guessing.
  • The ElevenLabs API is billed separately per 1,000 characters (pay as you go) and isn't mixed in here.

Tools left out

  • Murf: its pricing page shows only pay-as-you-go API rates ($0.03 per 1,000 characters), not a monthly plan quota.
  • Speechify: it's a read-aloud app with unlimited listening, not a voice-generation quota.
  • Descript: its plans count media minutes for editing and transcription, not a text-to-speech character quota.
  • Music tools (Suno, Udio) and transcription tools (Otter) do a different job.
