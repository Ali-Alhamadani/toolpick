What does a minute of AI video really cost?

AI video plans are sold in credits, and each model burns credits at a different rate per second. Enter how much finished video you need a month and see which plan you'd actually need, and the real price per minute, from each tool's official pricing page.

Why this exists: people regularly report running out of credits after a few short clips, e.g. “About… FIVE SHORT (<10s) videos later, i ran out of my credits for the month” (r/generativeAI) and “a short 15sec vid. It uses 70% of my credits in one go” (r/runwayml).

How we compare

  • Same model only. We only call one tool cheaper when both use the same model at the same resolution (for example Seedance 2.5 at 720p on Pika and Luma). Credit rates differ by model, so other combinations are shown but not ranked.
  • Monthly billing. All plan prices here are billed monthly.
  • Only what the pricing page shows. Where a page shows no top-up price, we say so instead of estimating. Credits can be used on other features too; this assumes all go to video.
  • Not included yet: Kling AI (we couldn't load its pricing page), and HeyGen (its pricing page doesn't show credits per minute). Avatar tools like Synthesia do a different job.
