How the watchlist works

When you add a tool we remember the price we had checked at that moment. Each visit compares that with our latest checked price and flags any change; “Mark all as seen” resets the comparison to today’s prices. “Cheaper for the same job” only compares tools that do the same main job, monthly with monthly and billed-yearly with billed-yearly. We never guess a price: tools without a checked US-dollar price say so.
