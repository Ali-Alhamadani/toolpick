If you can wait: plans with unlimited slow images

After the fast credits run out, these plans keep generating images in a slower queue at no extra cost, so a heavy month costs the same as a light one:

  • Ideogram Plus, $20/mo: “Slow credits: Unlimited” on Plus and Pro (source for Ideogram (opens in a new tab)).
  • Midjourney Standard, $30/mo: “Relax GPU Time: Unlimited Images” on Standard, Pro and Mega (source for Midjourney (opens in a new tab)).
  • Leonardo Premium, $30/mo: “Image Generation for select models at a relaxed pace: Unlimited” (15 Leonardo and FLUX models) (source for Leonardo (opens in a new tab)).
  • Krea Max, $105/mo: “Unlimited relaxed generations” with supported in-house image models (source for Krea (opens in a new tab)).

How we compare

  • Same unit. Every plan is turned into images per month for the model you pick.
  • Same model for “cheaper”. We only say one tool is cheaper when both run the same model (Ideogram 4.0 Turbo is on Ideogram and Krea; Recraft V4.1 Flash is on Recraft and Krea). Krea doesn't state the resolution for Ideogram 4.0; Ideogram's own rate is for 2K.
  • Monthly billing only. Yearly plans are cheaper per month but paid up front; see Monthly or yearly?
  • Only what the pages show. Top-ups are added only where the price is on the official page (Midjourney $4 per GPU hour, Recraft $2 per 200 credits, Ideogram pay as you go at 1¢ per credit). Otherwise we say “More than the biggest plan” instead of guessing.
  • Approximate. Midjourney's GPU minutes per prompt are its own averages; Krea's counts are rounded on its page. Editing, upscaling and reference images cost extra on most tools.

Tools left out

  • Leonardo: its pricing page doesn't say how many tokens an image uses (it depends on the model and settings), so we can't turn 8,500 tokens into images.
  • Magnific: plans mix yearly credit pools and “unlimited” models that change by plan and billing period, so there's no clear monthly image count to compare.
  • ChatGPT: its pricing page only says “Limited”, “More” or “Unlimited” image creation, with no number.
  • Canva: its pricing page describes AI as a shared allowance (“10x more AI than Canva Free”) with no image count.
  • Photoroom: it edits product photos (backgrounds, scenes) rather than selling image-generation credits.

FAQ

How much does one AI image cost?

On the entry plans we checked on 2026-10-31, from well under 1 cent (Krea 2 Turbo on Krea Basic: about 2,500 images for $9) to about 14 cents (Recraft V4.1 Pro Vector on Recraft Basic: 83 images for $12). The calculator works out your own cost from how many images you keep and how many tries each one takes.

How many images do I get with Midjourney Basic?

Midjourney Basic ($10/mo) gives 200 minutes of Fast GPU time. One standard (SD) prompt makes 4 images and uses about 0.8 minutes, so that's about 250 prompts or 1,000 images a month, or about 615 HD images. Midjourney says these are approximate.

Which AI image plans are unlimited?

Some plans let you keep generating in a slower queue after your fast credits run out: Ideogram Plus ($20/mo, unlimited slow credits), Midjourney Standard ($30/mo, unlimited Relax images), Leonardo Premium ($30/mo, unlimited relaxed images on selected models) and Krea Max ($105/mo, unlimited relaxed with supported in-house models). Choose “I can wait” in the calculator to count them.

Is pay as you go cheaper than a subscription?

For small amounts it can be. Ideogram's pay-as-you-go rate is 1¢ per credit, so 300 Ideogram 4.0 Turbo images (3 credits each) cost about $9, less than Plus at $20/mo. Above about 2,000 credits a month, Plus is cheaper, and it adds unlimited slow generation.

Why do two tools charge differently for the same model?

Krea, Recraft and others resell models like Ideogram 4.0 and Recraft V4.1 Flash. Each sets its own credit price, so we compare them only when the same model is picked in both cards. Prices checked 2026-10-31 on each official page.
