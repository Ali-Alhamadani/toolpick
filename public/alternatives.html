FAQ

How does ToolPick choose alternatives?

Tools with the same main job come first, and only they get a 'cheaper' verdict (monthly with monthly, yearly with yearly). Then close alternatives: tools that also do that job or that ToolPick ranks for the same job. If there are fewer than 3, other tools in the same category are listed and labelled as a different main job.

Where do the facts come from?

Prices from each official pricing page; free plans, AI training on your data, refunds and student deals from official pricing, privacy, help and student pages. Every fact shows its source link and the date we checked it. Facts we haven't checked show 'not checked' and are never counted as a match.

What does 'More private' mean?

The tool's official privacy pages say it doesn't train AI models on your content by default, or only if you opt in.

How we pick · Sponsor ToolPick · Newsletter · Embed price cards · llms.txt

Some links may become affiliate links in future; that never changes our picks. Prices copied from each tool's official pricing page; every tool shows its own checked date and pricing link (USD unless shown; yearly-billing prices in parentheses). "check site" = we could not verify the price, so we don't guess. Prices change — always confirm on the official page.
