People actively choosing an AI tool: creators, marketers, students, developers and small-business owners. They arrive from "best AI X tool", "X vs Y" and "free alternative to X" searches, so intent is high.

## Recommended structure: one simple ladder
| Tier | Placement | Launch price |
|---|---|---|
| **Category Sponsor** | Labelled slot on 1 category page + its "best free" page + matching job pages | **$99 / month** |
| **Sitewide Sponsor** (max 1 at a time) | Labelled slot on every page (230+ pages) + the category slot | **$299 / month** |
Newsletter placements are deferred until the newsletter exists.

**Why this:** two tiers keep the sales conversation simple for a new site with no traffic proof yet. Category sponsorship matches how buyers think (a voice-AI brand wants the audio pages) and is cheap enough for a first "yes". The single sitewide slot creates scarcity and a clear upgrade path. Prices are deliberately low and month-to-month. Re-price after 30 days of Analytics/Search Console data, targeting an effective CPM of $15-30. Launch incentive: 50% off the first month for the first 3 sponsors, in exchange for a testimonial.
